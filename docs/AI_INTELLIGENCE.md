# AUREL Intelligence (Contextual Personal AI)

**FigCraft Location**: Page 06 (Aurel Intelligence) + Page 10 (`Dark 06`, `Dark 07`)
**Completed Screens**: 5 Light/Voice screens (`25`–`29`) + 2 Midnight Navy Dark Mode screens

---

## 1. Design Philosophy: Calm, Blue, and Strictly Opt-In

Aurel Intelligence rejects the industry cliché of glowing purple chatbots and opaque background data harvesting.

- **Visual Signature**: Anchored in AUREL Signature Blue (`#0866FF`), Crystal Blue (`#9BCBFF`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), and Midnight Navy (`#080E1C`).
- **Explicit Consent First**: Screen `25` leads directly below the greeting with *"Private by default · Accesses only threads & files you select."*
- **Transparent Scope Indicators**: Every AI output names its source scope (`4 SAVED POSTS · OPT-IN` on Screen `26`; `Authorized Sources: 2 Spaces, Saved Posts · Private DMs Excluded` in green `#16865C` on Screen `29`).
