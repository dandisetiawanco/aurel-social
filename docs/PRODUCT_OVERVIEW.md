# AUREL Product Overview

**Product Name**: AUREL
**Tagline**: Beyond Connection.
**Brand Statement**: Connect with people. Discover more. Think together.
**Creative Production**: NGOPREK DIGITAL ([ngoprekdigital.site](https://www.ngoprekdigital.site/))
**Status**: Completed Product Design Concept and Interactive Mobile Prototype

---

## 1. Product Vision

AUREL unifies five daily consumer mobile behaviors inside one calm, iOS-first interface:

1. **Private Communication (`Aurel Chats`)**: Direct conversations, group workspaces, voice notes with synced transcripts, audio/video calls, and structured shared media.
2. **Editorial Social Discovery (`Aurel Social`)**: Photography-led feeds, edge-to-edge stories, asymmetric Explore curation, threaded discussions, and clean creator profiles.
3. **Immersive Short Video (`Aurel Clips`)**: Full-bleed 9:16 cinema presented behind translucent Dark Liquid Glass controls with discovery grids and creator publishing tools.
4. **Opt-In Contextual Intelligence (`Aurel Intelligence`)**: Personal assistant, live voice brainstorming orb, on-request media analysis, and scoped morning digests that exclude private direct messages by default.
5. **Focused Communities (`Aurel Spaces`)**: Topic groups, broadcast channels, pinned announcements, and community event RSVPs.

## 2. Target Audience & Core Principles

AUREL is conceived for design-literate everyday communicators, creative teams, visual storytellers, and community hosts who want fewer context switches without sacrificing visual calm or data boundaries.

- **Clarity over chrome**: Reading surfaces remain pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or midnight navy (`#080E1C`); Liquid Glass translucency is reserved strictly for floating navigation, composers, sheets, and media overlays.
- **Explicit user control over AI**: Aurel Intelligence never auto-sends messages, never reads private direct chats silently, and surfaces disclosure toggles wherever AI edits or captions assist published media.
- **Original brand identity**: Anchored by Signature Blue (`#0866FF`), Crystal Blue (`#9BCBFF`), and geometric A-arch iconography rather than generic purple AI aesthetics.
