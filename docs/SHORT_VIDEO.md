## 2. Cinema-First Overlay Ergonomics

- **Smoked Dark Glass over video**: All controls floating above 9:16 video frames use `#111B2CC4`–`#111B2CE6` translucent dark glass with `#FFFFFF33` specular hairlines so bright highlights in video frames never wash out icon legibility.
- **Controlled scrim placement**: Linear gradients apply strictly to the top `150 px` (status bar & switcher) and bottom `240 px` (caption & nav), preserving 100% of the central video frame unobstructed.
- **Viewer transparency**: Auto-generated captions trigger an explicit `AI-Assisted Disclosure Badge` toggle before publishing on Screen `24`.
