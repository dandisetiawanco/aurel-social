## 2. Editorial Composition Principles

- **Photography leads the frame**: Cards avoid heavy drop shadows, noisy gradients, and cluttered engagement badges so visual work holds primary focus.
- **Intentional asymmetry in Explore**: Screen `17` pairs a full-width curated banner above a tall vertical Clip tile (`180 x 278 px`) and two stacked square tiles (`180 x 133 px`), avoiding repetitive uniform grids.
- **Transparent AI authoring**: Screen `18` includes an explicit `AI-Assisted Label Disclosure` switch alongside accessibility-focused alt-text suggestions.
